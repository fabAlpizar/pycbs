Basis Sets

Overview

The program supports the following Gaussian basis sets. All names are case-insensitive and correspond to the internal hierarchical number definitions used for extrapolation.

Supported Basis Sets

Subminimal Basis Sets

Pople Basis Sets

  • STO-2G

  • STO-3G

  • STO-6G

  • 3-21G

  • 3-21++G

  • 3-21GSP

  • 4-22GSP

  • 4-31G

Huzinaga Basis set

  • MINI

  • MINI-s

  • MIDI

  • MIDI!

Minimal Basis set

Double-zeta Correlation-Consistent Dunning-type Basis set

  • VDZ (cc-pVDZ)

  • AVDZ (aug-cc-pVDZ)

  • d-AVDZ (d-aug-cc-pVDZ)

Extended Basis set

Correlation-Consistent Dunning-type Basis set

  • VTZ (cc-pVTZ)

  • AVTZ (aug-cc-pVTZ)

  • d-AVTZ (d-aug-cc-pVTZ)

  • VQZ (cc-pVQZ)

  • AVQZ (aug-cc-pVQZ)

  • d-AVQZ (d-aug-cc-pVQZ)

  • V5Z (cc-pV5Z)

  • AV5Z (aug-cc-pV5Z)

  • d-AV5Z (d-aug-cc-pV5Z)

  • V6Z (cc-pV6Z)

  • AV6Z (aug-cc-pV6Z)

  • V7T (cc-pV7Z)

F12-Optimized Basis Sets

  • VDZ-F12 (cc-pVDZ-F12-OptRI)

  • VTZ-F12 (cc-pVTZ-F12-OptRI)

  • VQZ-F12 (cc-pVQZ-F12-OptRI)
